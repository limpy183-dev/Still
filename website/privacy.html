Privacy & security

Your attention is yours. So is your data.

Still has no accounts and no analytics. Your selections, sessions and history stay on your PC, and we’re upfront about what the app can and can’t do.

0Accounts

Nothing to sign up for. Nothing to sign in to.

0Analytics & telemetry

Still doesn’t report how, when or whether you use it.

100%On your PC

Preferences, alerts, to-dos and history are stored locally.

What goes online

Only two things, and both are about what you asked for:

  • Website logos. For a website you select, Still requests only that domain’s icon from Google’s favicon service, then caches it locally. Offline sites get a letter icon.
  • Update check. Settings → Check for updates contacts GitHub, only when you click it. Nothing is sent about you.

The font is bundled. There are no ads, trackers or third-party SDKs.

What’s stored, and where

DataLocation
Preferences, to-dos, alerts%APPDATA%\still-focus (alerts in alerts.json, imported media in alert-media\)
Session state & history%ProgramData%\Still\state.json, written atomically and writable only by administrators and SYSTEM. Up to 500 recent sessions.
The guard%ProgramFiles%\Still Guard
The app%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Still

Uninstalling lets you choose to remove your data too. Export your history as JSON any time from Your progress.

App security

  • The Electron interface is sandboxed with context isolation and no Node integration.
  • A restrictive Content Security Policy; navigation is blocked.
  • Only allowlisted IPC methods, each with sender validation and bounded, validated input.

The guard

  • Still Guard runs as LocalSystem. Its named pipe is restricted to its owner, SYSTEM and administrators, with network access denied.
  • Requests are bounded and validated again inside the native service.
  • A policy snapshot is stored before any change. Cleanup removes only rules carrying Still’s reserved name prefix and restores the prior collection mode.
  • Unrelated rules, collections and concurrently added third-party rules are retained.

Browser companion

The companion uses Chrome and Edge’s native request rules. It doesn’t upload browsing history, and its native host waits on filesystem notifications rather than scanning tabs or polling URLs. For time limits, the companion checks the addresses of your open tabs every 30 seconds, on your PC only, and counts time while a limited website is open in any tab.

No TLS interception, root certificate, network proxy or DNS change is ever used.

Honest limits

Still is a focus aid, not an unbreakable lock. No app can guarantee that against the administrator of the same PC. We’d rather you know exactly where the edges are:

  • An administrator can stop or remove the service, change policy or the clock, or use another account.
  • Modified or differently identified binaries, another operating system and remote/streamed games aren’t covered.
  • Blocking a launcher doesn’t block every game it can start. Select the games too.
  • Website blocking covers Chrome and Edge. Other browsers and proxy/remote sites can bypass it.
  • The companion is unpacked; removing it bypasses immediate blocks until browser policies refresh.
  • Alerts need Still open or in the tray, and sleeping PCs aren’t woken.

Still shows these limits in the app, too. Essential Windows components, framework packages and Still itself can never be selected.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. To show the latest version and release notes, your browser asks GitHub’s public API for Still’s releases; the answer is kept in your browser for ten minutes. Downloads come straight from GitHub Releases.
