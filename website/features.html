Everything Still can do

Everything you need. Nothing that shouts.

A focus timer that means it, a calm library for your distractions, gentle website blocking, alerts, a little to-do list and a quiet record of your progress, all on your own PC.

01 · FOCUS SPACE

Settle into focus.

Set a duration, name what you’re making room for, and start. The timer, selection and pause are fixed until the session ends.

  • Any length from 1 to 1,440 minutes, with 25, 50 and 90-minute presets
  • An optional intention (“What would you like to make room for?”)
  • Selected running apps close when you start. Save your work first
  • Natural expiry always releases your apps, even mid-wait
  • Timers survive closing Still and restarting Windows

02 · APPS & WEBSITES

A quieter collection.

Still discovers what’s on your PC, so choosing is quick. Keep favourite combinations as groups: deep work, evenings, exam week.

  • Desktop apps, running windowed apps and eligible Microsoft Store packages
  • Browse directly to one or more .exe files
  • Paste a URL or type a domain; the whole domain and its subdomains are held
  • Website logos are fetched once and cached; offline sites get a letter icon
  • Essential Windows components and Still itself can’t be selected
For games, select the game’s own executable as well as its launcher. Blocking a launcher alone doesn’t cover every game it can start.

03 · WINDOWS PROTECTION

Blocked at launch. Not chased around.

Enable protection once. Windows asks for administrator approval and installs Still Guard, a small service that applies temporary AppLocker rules for the account that installed it.

  • Exact path and file-hash deny rules for desktop apps; renamed copies are recognised
  • Publisher/product rules cover signed updates where Windows allows
  • Store apps are matched by package identity across versions
  • Explorer, shortcuts, launchers and the command line are all covered
  • Only Still’s own rules are ever removed; other policies stay intact

04 · WEBSITE BLOCKING

A gentler way back to focus.

A small browser companion for Chrome and Edge puts selected websites on hold the moment a session starts, and turns already-open tabs into a calm block screen.

  • Three built-in screens: Quiet garden, Evening calm and A clean page
  • Make your own with a headline, a note to yourself and an image
  • Or redirect blocked visits to an HTTPS page you choose
  • Matches at label boundaries: blocking youtube.com leaves notyoutube.com alone
  • Browser policies add a fallback, including private and guest windows

05 · TIME LIMITS

Enough is enough.

Daily allowances and a bedtime for the websites that pull you in. They work any time, not just during focus sessions.

  • Add any website, or start with YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Reddit or X
  • Time counts while the site is open in any tab, checked every 30 seconds
  • One bedtime window (22:30–07:00 by default), switched on per website
  • When time is up, tabs go to a block page until midnight or the end of bedtime
  • Change limits whenever you like; they apply within a few seconds

06 · ALERTS

A gentle nudge. Or a fresh start.

Schedule a moment of intention once, daily or on weekdays. An alert can simply remind you, or open a focus window that blocks its own selection.

  • Full attention covers every display with sound; Focus card floats above your apps; Quiet reminder is a Windows notification
  • Four built-in sounds, silence, volume, or your own audio and video (up to 200 MB)
  • Images, GIFs and muted video banners on full-screen and card alarms
  • Snooze limits, a Show/Hide Dismiss option, and ranges that cross midnight
  • Recurring alarms follow local wall-clock time, including daylight saving

07 · TO-DO LIST

A little list. A clearer mind.

A keyboard-friendly list for your next steps. It saves as you type, including during a focus session.

  • Type / for a checkbox, tick circle, bullet, numbered item, heading or note
  • Keep typing to filter formats; ↑ ↓ and Enter to pick; Esc to dismiss
  • Enter for a new line; Backspace on an empty line removes it
  • Up to 500 lines of 2,000 characters each

08 · YOUR PROGRESS

Attention well spent.

A wider view of your focus: time, consistency, and what you made space for. No streak shaming, no leaderboards.

  • Total focus time, completed sessions and this week at a glance
  • 7, 30 and 90-day charts with a daily goal and a consistency grid
  • A session calendar: choose a day to see what you worked on
  • Search by intention, filter by outcome, archive, and export as JSON
